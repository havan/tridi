# Cable grommet Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. The user chose native execution with no subagents. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** `pixi run build grommet` produces a four-part, support-free grommet sized from a measured wire and a wall thickness.

**Architecture:** A new `projects/grommet/` following `projects/flange/`: `params.py` holds parameters, auto sizes and refusals, `parts.py` builds the solids, and `__init__.py` wires them to the shared export. The thread is a shared helper in `src/tridi/features.py`.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12, FreeCAD 1.1 (Part), pytest, pixi.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-grommet-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Coordinates: axis is z, z = 0 is the disc's outside face, +z points into the box. The split plane is y = 0, with `body_a` at y ≥ 0. A flat or twin wire's width runs along x.
- Parts are `body_a`, `body_b`, `nut_a`, `nut_b`, modelled in assembled position. The print rotation is identity for all four, so disc and nut's wall face are on the bed.
- Defaults: 5 × 2.5 mm `twin`, 2 mm wall. Expected sizes: slot 5.2 mm wide, thread 12.5 mm outside and 10.5 mm root, hole 13 mm, disc 24.5 × 2.5 mm, nut 7 mm tall.
- `grip_squeeze` 0.2 mm, `grip_wave` 0.2 mm, `groove_rib` 0.3 mm, clear zone 0.1 mm over wire height and 0.2 mm over wire width, thread pitch 2.5 mm and 1 mm deep with 45° flanks.
- Keep the test suite small: enough to catch wrong sizes, invalid solids and parts that collide, and no more.
- Work on branch `feat/grommet`. Commit messages carry no attribution lines.

## Review Focus

- A big `hole_d` for a connector: the slot stays the wire's size and the parts still build. Tested in Task 3.
- A thick wall, such as a 20 mm table: the stem lengthens and the grip stays under the nut. Tested in Task 3.
- A round wire too thick for two ridges in the nut's height: it still builds, with a note. Tested in Task 1.
- The nut's threads meeting the body's: assembled parts don't overlap. Tested in Task 3.
- Printing without supports: no large overhang on any default part. Tested in Task 3.

### Task 1: Parameters and sizing

**Files:**
- Create: `projects/grommet/__init__.py`, `projects/grommet/params.py`
- Test: `tests/grommet/__init__.py`, `tests/grommet/test_params.py`

**Interfaces:**

- [ ] **Step 1: Write failing tests** for: default sizes above; nut grows enough for the joint; `hole_d=20` grows thread, disc and nut but not the slot; `wall_thickness=20` gives stem end 2.5 + 20 + 7 + 1.5; a too-small `hole_d`, `grip_squeeze` over 15% of height, a `groove_rib` reaching mid-wire, and a too-tight `grip_pitch` are refused naming the parameter; `force=True` turns a refusal into a `forced:` note; `round` with `wire_d=10` gets a note about too few ridges.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run:** `pixi run pytest tests/grommet/test_params.py -q`. Expected: fail on import.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement.** Auto pitch is the larger of the smallest pitch keeping the bend radius at the wire's height or more and the ridge tops at 1 mm radius or more, and min(2 × height, `nut_height`). Ridges fit in the span: floor(2 × `nut_height` / pitch), at least 1, centred under the nut. Auto `nut_d` is the larger of thread + 8 mm and the joint's need (three 0.8 mm walls and tab), rounded up to 0.5 mm.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run the tests.** Expected: pass.
- [ ] **Step 5: Commit** "Grommet: parameters and sizing".

### Task 2: Thread helper

**Files:**
- Modify: `src/tridi/features.py`
- Test: `tests/test_features.py`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `threaded_rod(root_d: float, crest_d: float, pitch: float, z0: float, z1: float, phase_z: float = 0) -> Part.Shape`: a right-handed single-start trapezoid thread with 45° flanks, built by lofting one periodic spline section rotated along z, so a matching bore comes from the same call with bigger diameters.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write failing tests:** valid single solid; radial extent matches `crest_d` within 0.05 mm; volume lies between the root and crest cylinders; on a fixed radius mid-flank, material appears on one side at z and on the opposite side half a pitch up.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run:** `pixi run pytest tests/test_features.py -q -k threaded`. Expected: fail.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement.** Section radius follows the trapezoid in phase `u = frac(θ/2π − (z − phase_z)/pitch)`, lightly averaged to round the corners. 12 sections per turn, 96 points per section.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run the tests.** Expected: pass.
- [ ] **Step 5: Commit** "Add a threaded rod helper".

### Task 3: Parts

**Files:**
- Create: `projects/grommet/parts.py`, wire up `projects/grommet/__init__.py`
- Test: `tests/grommet/test_parts.py`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Task 1 helpers and `threaded_rod`.
- Produces: `build_body(p, side: str)`, `build_nut(p, clearance: float, side: str)`, and `build(p, printer)` returning four `PrintPart`s.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write failing tests:**
  - All four default parts are valid single solids.
  - Assembled parts don't overlap: common volume of each body/nut pair is under 1% of the nut's volume, and `body_a`/`body_b` meet with no overlap.
  - The nut's wall face is at z = 4.5 and the disc's inner face at z = 2.5, so a 2 mm wall is clamped.
  - At a ridge, the slot's gap on the axis is `wire_height − grip_squeeze` within 0.05 mm, and in the clear zone `wire_height + 0.1`.
  - On `twin`, the rib is present on the slot's face at x = 0 in the clear zone.
  - `hole_d=20`, `wall_thickness=20` and `round` with `wire_d=6` each build valid parts.
  - In print orientation, no default part has more than 5 mm² of mesh facing down steeper than 50° from vertical, away from the bed. The socket roof is a short bridge and is exempt for `nut_b`.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run:** `pixi run pytest tests/grommet/test_parts.py -q`. Expected: fail.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement the body:** disc with 0.4 mm bed chamfer and a scalloped edge that avoids the split, plus `threaded_rod`, minus the slot, cut to its half. The slot is the wire's section in clear and grip zones, trimmed by a wavy band across the grip span, with flared mouths capped at 45°. A V-shaped key and socket in the disc on each side line the halves up along the axis.
- [ ] **Step 4: Implement the nut:** scalloped ring minus a bore from `threaded_rod` with diameters grown by twice the clearance, split at x = 0. `nut_a` carries a T tab at +y and −y and `nut_b` the sockets. **Spec change:** tabs start at the bed and sockets are closed at the top with a short bridged roof, because a socket closed at the bed end leaves the tab overhanging in mid-air. Update the spec to match.
- [ ] **Step 5: Run the tests.** Expected: pass. Inspect a build visually with a section render.
- [ ] **Step 6: Commit** "Grommet: body and nut parts".
